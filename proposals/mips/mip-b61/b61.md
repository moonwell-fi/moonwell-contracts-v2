# MIP-B61: Align Base ETH Derivative Oracles with the WETH OEV Feed

## Summary

This proposal makes Moonwell's Base Core markets for cbETH, wstETH, rETH, weETH,
and wrsETH read ETH/USD from the same OEV wrapper that prices WETH.

Today WETH is priced through the ETH/USD OEV wrapper, which serves the previous
Chainlink round for up to 10 seconds after each new round. The five ETH
derivative markets are priced by composite oracles that read the ETH/USD feed
directly and always see the newest round. Inside each 10-second window the
protocol therefore values ETH at two different prices in the same block.

## Motivation

Liquidations compare the price of the borrowed asset to the price of the
collateral. When a borrower owes WETH and posts an ETH derivative as collateral,
the two prices come from different ETH/USD rounds during the delay window. When
ETH is falling, the liquidator seizes more collateral than the 10% liquidation
incentive allows. The excess is about 1.10 times the ETH/USD move between
rounds, applied to the amount repaid. The same mismatch can also make a borrower
close to the liquidation threshold liquidatable when neither consistent price
would.

The issue was reported through the Moonwell bug bounty and confirmed on a Base
fork. Principal is never at risk. The impact is a bounded transfer from the
borrower to the liquidator during the delay window.

When both legs read the same ETH/USD source, the delay applies equally to the
borrowed asset and the collateral, and the difference cancels out.

## Proposal

This proposal will:

1. Deploy new composite oracles for cbETH, wstETH, rETH, weETH, and wrsETH whose
   ETH/USD input is the ETH/USD OEV wrapper that prices WETH. Each new oracle
   keeps the exchange-rate feeds of the composite it replaces.
2. Point the cbETH, wstETH, rETH, weETH, and wrsETH feeds in the Base Chainlink
   oracle at the new composite oracles.
3. Archive the replaced composite oracle addresses for reference.

This proposal depends on MIP-X72, which deploys the ETH/USD OEV wrapper used
here. It must execute after MIP-X72.

## Markets Impacted

| Market | Composite oracle replaced                 |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| cbETH  | `cbETH_COMPOSITE_ORACLE`                  |
| wstETH | `CHAINLINK_WSTETH_STETH_COMPOSITE_ORACLE` |
| rETH   | `CHAINLINK_RETH_ETH_EXCHANGE_RATE_ORACLE` |
| weETH  | `CHAINLINK_WEETH_USD_COMPOSITE_ORACLE`    |
| wrsETH | `CHAINLINK_wrsETH_COMPOSITE_ORACLE`       |

## Considerations

Outside the 10-second window prices are unchanged. Inside it, the five markets
now see the same delayed ETH/USD round as WETH, instead of the newest one.

These markets have no OEV wrapper of their own, so a liquidation that seizes one
of them as collateral cannot pay for early access to the new round. Such a
liquidation waits at most 10 seconds for the window to close.

This proposal does not change collateral factors, liquidation incentives,
interest rates, OEV fee settings, or supported assets.

## Voting Options

- **Yes:** Price the Base ETH derivative markets from the same ETH/USD OEV feed
  as WETH.
- **No:** Keep the current oracle configuration.
- **Abstain:** Neutral.
