# MIP-X63: Anthias Labs Monthly Risk Parameter (8/2026) and Reserve Recommendations August 2026

## Summary

MIP-x63 seeks to implement risk parameter recommendations as part of Anthias'
Labs monthly parameter recommendations for the month of August 2026, and to
execute the Reserve Recommendations for August 2026: using protocol reserves to
repay bad debt held by insolvent accounts (accounts supplying less than $1.00 of
assets) on Base and OP Mainnet.

## Bad Debt Repayment from Reserves

For each market below, reserves are withdrawn (`_reduceReserves`) and used to
repay debt in the same asset via `repayBorrowBehalf` on behalf of insolvent
borrower accounts. Reserves are only used to repay debt in the same
asset/market; when reserves cannot cover all eligible debt, eligible borrowers
receive the same pro-rata coverage percentage.
