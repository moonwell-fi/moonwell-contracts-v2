## Moonbeam stkWELL Cooldown Removal

As part of the Moonbeam deprecation, this proposal also sets the stkWELL
withdrawal cooldown on Moonbeam to 0 (previously 7 days) via
`setCoolDownSeconds(0)` on the Safety Module (executed by the Temporal Governor,
its emissions manager). Stakers can activate the cooldown and redeem
immediately; the 2-day unstake window is unchanged.

## Funding Note

The Ethereum MRD funding transfer is clamped 1 WELL below the worker-generated
amount (4,160,470.94 instead of 4,160,471.94 WELL): the generated epoch outflow
slightly exceeded the Foundation's live xWELL balance due to floating-point
rounding in the automation output. Reward speeds are unchanged; the 1 WELL
difference is covered by the MRD's existing balance buffer.
