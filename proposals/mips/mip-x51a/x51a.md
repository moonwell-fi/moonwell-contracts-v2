# MIP-X51A: Monthly Rewards Distribution — Moonbeam + Optimism

Split from MIP-X51 to fit Moonbeam's per-extrinsic gas ceiling. This half covers
Moonbeam and Optimism reward updates for the April 2026 distribution period.

Base chain rewards and the four bad-debt repayments from the original MIP-X51
ship separately as **MIP-X51B**.

## Scope

- **Moonbeam (1284)**: 2 bridge-to-recipient + 5 transferFrom + 16
  setRewardSpeed updates, plus stkWELL emission rate.
- **Optimism (10)**: 13 setMRDSpeeds updates + 2 transferFrom + stkWELL emission
  rate, delivered cross-chain via one Wormhole `publishMessage`.

No Base actions, no bad-debt repayments. Those are in MIP-X51B.

## Motivation

The original MIP-X51 propose() transaction measured at 36.2M gas, which exceeds
Moonbeam's effective per-extrinsic weight budget (~25-30M empirical). After
RPC-layer submit caps on every public provider also refused the transaction,
splitting along chain-destination was the lowest-friction remediation. This
split keeps the rewards-automation semantics intact and only changes how the
actions are bundled into governance proposals.
