# MIP-X58: Moonwell Governance on Ethereum

## Summary

This proposal migrates Moonwell’s core governance system from Moonbeam to
Ethereum Mainnet.

If approved, Moonwell will deploy and activate a new MultichainGovernorV2 on
Ethereum, update the supporting governance infrastructure on Moonbeam, Base, and
Optimism, and connect voting power across chains through a new
VotingPowerAggregator system.

This is a major milestone for Moonwell. Governance is the system that allows
WELL tokenholders to propose, vote on, and execute changes across the protocol.
Moving the primary governor to Ethereum Mainnet places Moonwell governance on
the most widely used smart contract settlement layer, while preserving
Moonwell’s multichain design across Base, Optimism, Moonbeam, and Ethereum.

At a high level, this proposal:

- Moves the primary Moonwell governor from Moonbeam to Ethereum Mainnet
- Deploys MultichainGovernorV2 on Ethereum
- Deploys new VotingPowerAggregator contracts to better track voting power
  across supported chains
- Upgrades vote collection contracts on Base and Optimism
- Deploys a Moonbeam TemporalGovernor so Moonbeam contracts can still be
  governed by the new Ethereum-based system
- Updates trusted cross-chain governance connections across Ethereum, Moonbeam,
  Base, and Optimism
- Transfers ownership and admin control of relevant Moonbeam contracts from the
  old governor to the new governance structure
- Adds improvements that make large proposals easier to submit and governance
  execution safer

## Background

Moonwell governance was originally centered on Moonbeam. This made sense during
Moonwell’s earlier stages, but the protocol has since expanded across multiple
chains, including Base and Optimism, with Ethereum becoming increasingly
important to the WELL ecosystem.

As Moonwell continues to grow, the governance system needs to support a broader
multichain future. This includes the ability for WELL, xWELL, and stkWELL voting
power to be recognized across supported chains, and for governance decisions to
be executed reliably across Moonbeam, Base, Optimism, and Ethereum.

The new governance architecture keeps Moonwell multichain, but moves the main
proposal and voting hub to Ethereum Mainnet. In the new system, users vote
through the Ethereum governor, while supporting contracts on other chains
collect voting power and help execute approved proposals through Wormhole
messages.

This proposal also includes several practical improvements to the governance
system. Large proposals can be split into smaller pieces before being finalized,
proposal descriptions can be referenced through a URI instead of storing the
full text onchain, and passed proposals must be executed within a defined
execution window instead of remaining executable indefinitely.

## Proposal

If approved, this proposal will migrate Moonwell’s primary governance system
from Moonbeam to Ethereum Mainnet.

The proposal will deploy and activate MultichainGovernorV2 on Ethereum, making
Ethereum the new home for Moonwell governance proposals, voting, and execution
coordination. It will also deploy supporting VotingPowerAggregator contracts so
user voting power can be recognized across Moonwell’s supported governance
assets and chains.

To complete the migration, the proposal will update Moonbeam, Base, and Optimism
so they recognize the new Ethereum governor as the source of Moonwell governance
instructions. On Moonbeam, a TemporalGovernor will be used to continue managing
Moonbeam protocol contracts after the primary governor moves to Ethereum. On
Base and Optimism, the vote collection contracts will be upgraded to work with
the new governor and voting power system.

## Rationale

This is one of the most important governance upgrades in Moonwell’s history.

Moonwell is no longer a single-chain protocol. The protocol now operates across
multiple ecosystems, and governance needs to reflect that reality. Moving the
primary governor to Ethereum Mainnet gives Moonwell a stronger long-term
governance foundation while maintaining the ability to govern contracts across
Moonbeam, Base, Optimism, and Ethereum.

This migration is important for several reasons.

First, it places Moonwell governance on Ethereum Mainnet, the most established
smart contract settlement layer. This improves the long-term credibility and
neutrality of Moonwell’s governance system.

Second, it better aligns governance with the broader WELL ecosystem. As xWELL
and stkWELL become more important across chains, governance needs a cleaner way
to recognize voting power from multiple sources.

Third, it improves the protocol’s ability to handle large and complex governance
actions. Moonwell governance proposals often need to coordinate actions across
several chains. MultichainGovernorV2 makes this easier by allowing large
proposals to be assembled in smaller pieces before final submission.

Fourth, it keeps Moonwell’s multichain architecture intact. The proposal does
not abandon Moonbeam, Base, or Optimism. Instead, it creates a cleaner structure
where Ethereum serves as the primary governance hub, and the other chains
continue to participate through vote collection and execution contracts.

Finally, this proposal gives Moonwell a more future-proof governance system. By
separating voting power into its own aggregator contract, Moonwell can more
easily support future voting sources and governance improvements without
overloading the governor itself.

## Safety and Risk Considerations

This proposal has been designed as a careful migration rather than a simple
contract replacement. The migration includes a new Ethereum governor, new voting
power infrastructure, updated vote collection contracts, and ownership transfers
across multiple chains. Because this touches core governance infrastructure, it
is a significant proposal and should be reviewed carefully by voters and
technical contributors.

The migration also includes emergency “break-glass” functionality that can help
unwind cross-chain trust relationships if needed. This is intended as a safety
mechanism during a major governance transition.

## Voting Options

**For:** Approve MIP-X58 and migrate Moonwell governance from Moonbeam to
Ethereum Mainnet using the MultichainGovernorV2 architecture. **Against:** Do
not approve MIP-X58. Moonwell governance will remain centered on the existing
Moonbeam governor, and the proposed Ethereum governance migration will not be
executed. **Abstain:** Participate in the governance process without voting for
or against the proposal.
