# MIP-X66: Anthias Labs Monthly Risk Parameter and Reserve Recommendations September 2026

## Summary

MIP-x66 seeks to implement risk parameter recommendations as part of Anthias'
Labs monthly parameter recommendations for the month of September 2026, and to
execute the Reserve Recommendations for September 2026: using protocol reserves to
repay bad debt held by insolvent accounts on Base.
