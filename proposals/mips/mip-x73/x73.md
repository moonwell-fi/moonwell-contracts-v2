# MIP-X73: mToken Internal Cash Accounting Upgrade

## Summary

This proposal upgrades Moonwell's active mToken markets on Ethereum, Base, and
OP Mainnet to use internal cash accounting.

Today, mToken markets determine their available cash from the token balance held
by the market contract. This means a direct token transfer to a market can
increase the balance without going through Moonwell's normal supply flow.

The proposed upgrade changes this behavior so only assets transferred through
normal protocol operations are counted as market cash. Direct token transfers
will no longer increase an mToken's exchange rate or count toward supply-cap
accounting.

## Motivation

Supply caps are intended to limit how much exposure Moonwell can take on in a
given market. For these protections to work as intended, market cash and
exchange-rate accounting should only reflect assets that enter or leave through
Moonwell's protocol functions.

Under the current implementation, tokens sent directly to an mToken contract are
included in its cash balance even though the transfer does not pass through the
normal supply checks. This can distort market accounting and reduce the
effectiveness of supply-cap protections.

This proposal strengthens mToken accounting by tracking market cash internally.
Mints, repayments, redemptions, borrows, and other normal protocol transfers
will continue to update market cash as expected, while unsolicited direct
transfers will be excluded from protocol accounting.

## Proposal

This proposal will:

1. Deploy updated mToken implementations on Ethereum, Base, and OP Mainnet.
2. Upgrade all current mToken markets registered with the Comptroller on each
   network to the new implementation.
3. Upgrade the WETH market implementation on each network so WETH follows the
   same internal cash accounting model while preserving its existing native ETH
   functionality.
4. Upgrade the Base WETH admin wrapper so governance can perform the WETH
   implementation upgrade and manage any excess WETH sent directly to the
   market.
5. Update the repository's canonical implementation addresses as soon as the new
   implementations are deployed, before this proposal is submitted, so any
   market listed afterwards, including while this proposal is being voted on,
   uses the same accounting model. Such a market is already on the new
   implementation and is left unchanged by this proposal.

## Networks and Markets Affected

The upgrade applies to all current Moonwell mToken markets on:

- Ethereum
- Base
- OP Mainnet

This includes the WETH market on each network.

No Moonbeam or Moonriver markets are included in this proposal.

## How the New Accounting Works

After the upgrade, each mToken will maintain an internally tracked cash balance.

This balance will increase when assets enter through normal Moonwell operations
and decrease when assets leave through normal Moonwell operations. A market's
reported cash is the lower of this internal balance and the tokens it actually
holds.

Apart from the loss case described below, tokens transferred directly to an
mToken contract outside of these flows will not:

- Increase the market's reported cash
- Increase the mToken exchange rate
- Count toward supply-cap accounting
- Increase a user's collateral value

Governance will also have the ability to sweep assets held by a market in excess
of its internally accounted cash. Only the excess amount can be swept, so this
function cannot reduce the cash accounted as backing the market.

If a market loses tokens outside normal protocol operations (for example, an
issuer seizing or burning tokens), its reported cash falls with its actual token
balance. The loss is reflected in the exchange rate immediately and shared by
all suppliers in proportion to their supply. If the tokens are later returned,
the market recovers automatically, up to its accounted cash and no further.

Governance can also lower a market's accounted cash to its actual token balance.
This only makes a loss permanent, so that returned tokens no longer restore it,
and governance should use it only once a loss is known to be permanent. This
function can never increase accounted cash.

In the unlikely case that a permanent loss removes nearly all of a market's
supplier equity, the market can stop accruing interest, which blocks activity in
that market until governance restores it through a dedicated recovery procedure.

When a market's implementation is upgraded in the future with the resign step
enabled (`allowResign = true`, which the repository requires for these markets),
any tokens held above its accounted cash are first transferred to the market
admin, so they are not absorbed into the exchange rate. An upgrade made without
that step absorbs such tokens into the exchange rate.

Existing Moonwell ERC-4626 vaults on Base are not upgraded by this proposal.
They still read market cash from the token balance, so their share price can be
affected by direct transfers to a market until the excess is swept by governance
or the vaults are redeployed. These vaults are small: five in total, each under
0.1% of its market.

## Voting Options

- **Yes:** Approve the mToken implementation upgrades and enable internal cash
  accounting across Moonwell markets on Ethereum, Base, and OP Mainnet.
- **No:** Keep the current mToken cash-accounting implementation.
- **Abstain:** Neutral.

## Conclusion

MIP-X73 strengthens Moonwell's market accounting by ensuring that only assets
entering and leaving through normal protocol operations affect mToken cash,
exchange rates, and supply-cap accounting.

The upgrade applies across Moonwell's Ethereum, Base, and OP Mainnet deployments
while preserving existing market state and user positions.
