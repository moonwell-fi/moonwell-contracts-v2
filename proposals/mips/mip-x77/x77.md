# MIP-X77: Anthias Labs Risk Parameter Recommendations (11/11/26)

## Summary

This proposal implements Anthias Labs' November 11, 2026 risk parameter
recommendations for Moonwell Core markets on Base and OP Mainnet. It continues
the collateral factor wind-down for AERO and WELL on Base and for OP on OP
Mainnet, and steps down VVV's collateral factor on Base.

## Base

### Risk Parameter Changes

| Market | Parameter         |          Current |        Proposed |
| ------ | ----------------- | ---------------: | --------------: |
| AERO   | Collateral Factor |              55% |             45% |
| WELL   | Collateral Factor |              20% |             10% |
| VVV    | Collateral Factor |              50% |             45% |
| VVV    | Supply Cap        |      170,000 VVV |      10,000 VVV |
| weETH  | Supply Cap        |      5,400 weETH |       680 weETH |
| cbBTC  | Supply Cap        |      1,500 cbBTC |       460 cbBTC |
| WETH   | Supply Cap        |      96,000 WETH |     20,000 WETH |
| EURC   | Supply Cap        | 21,900,000 EURC | 1,500,000 EURC |

Supply cap changes will be executed through the Cap Guardian.

**AERO:** Legacy AERO secondary market liquidity has kept falling. A ~5%
price-impact sale reached about $1.2M on 10/26 and $938K on 11/9 ($688K against
the oracle price), now below the $738,207 of AERO posted by healthy borrowers.
45% is the deepest step that stops short of the largest AERO-only loan.

**WELL:** A ~5% price-impact sale fell from $21,662 on 10/26 to $15,000 on 11/9,
below the $17,346 of WELL posted by healthy borrowers. WELL has the highest
30-day volatility of the Base collateral reviewed (113.5%) and a 23.06% worst
24-hour decline over the last 90 days.

**VVV:** VVV is the most volatile Base collateral still at a 50% factor (101.7%
30-day / 123.5% 90-day volatility). A 45% collateral factor crosses no account.
The supply cap is reduced to 10,000 VVV as much of the current cap is
underutilized.

## OP Mainnet

### Risk Parameter Changes

| Market | Parameter         |         Current |       Proposed |
| ------ | ----------------- | --------------: | -------------: |
| OP     | Collateral Factor |             50% |            40% |
| WETH   | Borrow Cap        |     30,000 WETH |       700 WETH |
| USDC   | Borrow Cap        | 34,000,000 USDC | 1,500,000 USDC |
| USDT   | Borrow Cap        | 18,400,000 USDT |   500,000 USDT |
| USD₮0  | Borrow Cap        | 8,000,000 USD₮0 | 250,000 USD₮0 |

Borrow cap changes will be executed through the Cap Guardian.

**OP:** Very little borrowing depends on OP: healthy borrowers post only about
$4,900 of OP as collateral. A ~5% price-impact sale fell from $217,504 on 10/26
to $125K on 11/9, and OP's 30-day volatility is 125.0%. At 40%, 33 accounts
become newly eligible for liquidation with $393.52 of debt between them; only
seven owe more than $10 and the largest owes $94.70.

**Borrow caps:** Current USDC, USDT, USD₮0 and WETH borrow caps are 22–42× what
OP Mainnet DEX liquidity can absorb at ~5% price impact. The new caps are sized
to current measured depth.

Borrowers can avoid liquidation by repaying a small amount or adding collateral
before this proposal executes.
