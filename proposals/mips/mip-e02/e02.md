# MIP-E02: USDC & USDT IRM Adjustment and cbETH Remediation Round 3

This proposal seeks to adjust the interest rate model (IRM) curve for the USDC and USDT markets on Ethereum mainnet. At deployment, the multiplier parameter was set one order of magnitude higher than the intended value (0.67 instead of 0.067). This proposal corrects it to the target configuration.

## Proposed Changes

| **Parameter** | **Previous Value** | **Recommended Value** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base Rate | 0 | 0 |
| Multiplier | **0.67** | **0.067** |
| Jump Multiplier | 9 | 9 |
| Kink | 0.9 | 0.9 |

**Projected APY**

| Utilization | Borrow APY | Supply APY |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0% | 0% | 0% |
| 90% (kink) | 6.03% | 4.88% |
| 100% | 96.03% | 86.43% |

## Next cbETH Remediation Round

A third cbETH remediation repayment round totaling **146.78 ETH** is planned. Allocations are calculated proportionally from each wallet’s outstanding remediation balance after the first two rounds. Repayments for wallets pending KYC remain on hold, while KYC-cleared wallets use their approved recipient addresses.

Details of the planned round can be viewed on the [cbETH Remediation Dashboard](https://cbeth-remediation.vercel.app/).
