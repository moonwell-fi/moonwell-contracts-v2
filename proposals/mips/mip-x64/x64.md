# MIP-X64: Moonbeam Sunset — "Before Sunset" Phase

## Summary

This proposal begins the orderly sunset of Moonwell's Moonbeam deployment
(Wormhole chain id `16`). It is the **"Before Sunset"** phase: it removes the
routes that point **to** Moonbeam so that no new governance messages or `xWELL`
bridge transfers are sent to a chain that is being wound down.

Concretely, MIP-X64 does two things:

1. **Removes Moonbeam from Moonwell governance.** The Ethereum
   `MultichainGovernorV2` stops broadcasting proposals to the Moonbeam vote
   collector and stops accepting vote-collection messages from Moonbeam.

2. **Blocks bridging `xWELL` TO Moonbeam.** The `WormholeBridgeAdapter` on
   Ethereum, Base, and Optimism has its outbound route to Moonbeam cleared.

Crucially, **bridging OUT of Moonbeam remains fully enabled.** Moonbeam holders
can continue to move their `xWELL` to Ethereum, Base, or Optimism. Nothing on
Moonbeam is modified by this proposal.

To be explicit about the governance impact: **once this proposal executes, WELL
and stkWELL held on Moonbeam can no longer be used to vote on Moonwell
governance proposals.** Holders who wish to keep voting should bridge their WELL
to Ethereum, Base, or Optimism — the exit routes this proposal deliberately
keeps open.

This proposal moves no tokens, upgrades no contracts, and changes no bridge rate
limits. It only removes routing configuration.

## Background

Moonwell governance was migrated from Moonbeam to Ethereum in MIP-X58, and
Ethereum was connected to the `xWELL` bridge mesh in MIP-X55. With governance
now anchored on Ethereum and the Moonbeam markets being retired, the Moonbeam
leg of the cross-chain topology is no longer needed.

Because this is a **sunset** rather than an abrupt shutdown, it is split into
phases so that users are never trapped:

- **Before Sunset (this proposal, MIP-X64):** stop sending anything _to_
  Moonbeam, while leaving every path _out of_ Moonbeam open.
- **After Sunset (a later proposal):** once outflows have wound down, perform
  the final decommissioning of the Moonbeam-side contracts.

## Motivation

Continuing to broadcast governance proposals to Moonbeam and continuing to
advertise Moonbeam as a valid bridge destination adds cost and operational risk
for a chain that is being retired. Severing the inbound routes now — while
keeping exits open — lets holders migrate on their own timeline and cleanly
prepares the protocol for final decommissioning.

## Proposal Actions

MIP-X64 is executed by the Ethereum `MultichainGovernorV2`. The Ethereum actions
run directly; the Base and Optimism actions are delivered to each chain's
`TemporalGovernor` via Wormhole.

### Ethereum

1. **`MultichainGovernorV2.removeExternalChainConfigs([{16, Moonbeam vote collector}])`**

   Removes Moonbeam (Wormhole chain id `16`) from the governor's external chain
   configuration. This single action:

   - stops the governor from broadcasting future proposals to the Moonbeam vote
     collector, and
   - stops the governor from accepting vote-collection messages from Moonbeam
     (the governor's trusted-sender check for a chain is exactly "is this the
     configured target address for that chain?", so clearing the target both
     removes the broadcast route and revokes trust in one step).

2. **`WormholeBridgeAdapter.setTargetAddresses([{16, address(0)}])`**

   Clears the Ethereum `xWELL` bridge adapter's outbound route to Moonbeam.
   Sends to Moonbeam now revert; receives from Moonbeam are unaffected.

### Base

3. **`WormholeBridgeAdapter.setTargetAddresses([{16, address(0)}])`** — clears
   the Base `xWELL` bridge adapter's outbound route to Moonbeam.

### Optimism

4. **`WormholeBridgeAdapter.setTargetAddresses([{16, address(0)}])`** — clears
   the Optimism `xWELL` bridge adapter's outbound route to Moonbeam.

## What Is Intentionally Left Untouched

- **Bridging OUT of Moonbeam stays enabled.** The `xWELL` adapters on Ethereum,
  Base, and Optimism keep Moonbeam in their trusted-sender lists, so bridge
  messages originating on Moonbeam are still accepted and minted. Only the
  send-to-Moonbeam direction is removed.
- **The Moonbeam-side contracts are not modified.** The Moonbeam
  `WormholeBridgeAdapter` retains its outbound routes to Ethereum, Base, and
  Optimism, so Moonbeam holders can still exit.
- **All non-Moonbeam routes are preserved.** The governor's Base and Optimism
  configuration and every non-Moonbeam bridge route are left exactly as they
  are.
- **The governance execution path TO Moonbeam remains intact.** This proposal
  only removes the vote-collection config; the Ethereum governor can still
  deliver executable actions to the Moonbeam `TemporalGovernor` via Wormhole.
  That path is how the "After Sunset" proposal will perform the final
  Moonbeam-side decommissioning.

## Execution Timing

Once this proposal executes, the governor immediately stops accepting
vote-collection messages from Moonbeam. Any _other_ proposal still inside its
cross-chain vote-collection window at that moment would silently lose Moonbeam
votes that have not yet been relayed. **MIP-X64 should therefore be queued and
executed only when no other proposal is in its cross-chain vote-collection
window.**

A subsequent **"After Sunset"** proposal will handle the final decommissioning
of the Moonbeam deployment once outflows have settled.

## Voting Options

**FOR:** Approve MIP-X64, removing the inbound routes to Moonbeam while keeping
all Moonbeam exit paths open, in preparation for the Moonbeam sunset.
**AGAINST:** Do not approve MIP-X64, leaving Moonbeam fully wired into
governance and the bridge mesh. **ABSTAIN:** Take no position on this proposal.
